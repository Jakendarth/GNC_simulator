# Organisations

GNC Simulator includes algorithms and results developed during Nikos Kougiatsos's involvement in the Safe Autonomous Maritime Transport research group at Delft University of Technology, as recorded in the repository's [NOTICE](assets/legal/NOTICE.txt).

## SAFE-NET

**Safe Autonomous Maritime Transport** is a TU Delft research group working on safe maritime autonomy. Its research combines modelling, control, monitoring and planning, with verification and validation of maritime systems.

[Visit SAFE-NET](https://www.safe-net.team/)
